Where The Town Ends And The Veld Begins.

The interface between settlement and open country is where a wildfire becomes a public safety problem. Watching it should not depend on someone phoning it in.

Book a live demo

Most ignitions are still reported by whoever sees them

That is the honest state of things in most municipal areas. It means detection depends on who happens to be awake, outdoors and facing the right way. Continuous thermal monitoring replaces that with something that does not blink, does not go off shift and covers the whole jurisdiction rather than the parts people drive through.

Shared awareness across members and boundaries

Fire Protection Associations coordinate people who work for different organisations and answer to different bosses. A single common picture of where fires are, updated the same way for everyone, removes a great deal of the friction in that coordination and stops two teams responding to the same column of smoke from opposite directions.

Built around how you already work

Your own boundaries define the monitored area, alerts arrive on the channels your people already use, and the data can push into the mapping systems you already run. Nobody is asked to abandon a process that works in order to adopt a new screen.
